3. Set up the phone

  1. Turn brightness all the way up. Turn off auto-brightness.
  2. Stop the screen from turning off. iPhone: open Settings › Display & Brightness › Auto-Lock › Never. Also turn off Low Power Mode. Android: open Settings › Display › Screen timeout › pick the longest. Also turn off battery saver.
  3. Turn the volume all the way up and plug the phone in to charge.
  4. Optional: put yellow or orange tape over the flashlight for a warmer glow.
  5. Tap Start, allow the microphone when asked, and put the phone in the pumpkin with the screen facing out.

To mute it, tap the screen or say "Mute on" (or "Pumpkin, stop talking"). To unmute, tap again or say "Mute off" (or "Pumpkin, unmute"). Tap Settings in the corner to change things.

The pumpkin's voice and wit come from Google (or OpenAI). A "key" is a long code, like a password, that lets your pumpkin use them. Google's is free.

How do I get a key?
  1. Open Google's free key page and sign in with any Google or Gmail account. Agree to the terms if it asks.
  2. Tap Create API key.
  3. Tap the copy button next to the long code.
  4. Come back here and paste it in the box above.
How do I get a key?
  1. Open OpenAI's key page and sign in.
  2. Tap Create new secret key, then copy it. It starts with sk-.
  3. Add a few dollars of credit under Billing. OpenAI isn't free: about a few cents per conversation.
  4. Come back here and paste it in the box above.

You can save both a Google and an OpenAI key and switch between them with the buttons above.

Privacy

  • When someone talks to the pumpkin, what they say goes to Google (or OpenAI, if you use an OpenAI key) so it can answer.
  • This website doesn't record or keep anything. Your key and settings stay on this phone.
  • With a free Google key, Google may use what it hears to improve its products.
  • If you buy the unlock, Stripe handles the payment. We keep only a scrambled fingerprint of your key with it, so your phones unlock automatically. We can't turn it back into your key.
  • If you came here from a Google ad, Google's ad tag notes whether that visit led to a purchase, so we can tell which ads work. It doesn't see what the pumpkin hears.
  • If you add an ElevenLabs key, the words the pumpkin says go to ElevenLabs so it can speak them in your voice.
  • The site counts anonymous usage, like page views and which options are used, with a random number stored on your phone. No names, no accounts, nothing it hears.
