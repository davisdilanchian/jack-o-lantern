Talking Jack-o'-Lantern

Turns this phone into a pumpkin that hears visitors and talks back, with its light flickering as it speaks.

Gemini key

Free with any Google account. Get a key from Google AI Studio. It stays on this phone and is only sent to Google.

Point this camera at the QR code on the other phone (Settings, Show key as QR).

Scan with the other phone's camera, then open the link. Anyone who scans this gets your key, so hide it when you're done.

Character

Light

The screen fades from black to orange to white as it talks. Flashlight control needs Chrome on Android; iPhones use the screen.

Before you set it out

  1. Brightness to max and auto-brightness off.
  2. Keep the screen on. iPhone: Settings › Display & Brightness › Auto-Lock › Never, and Low Power Mode off. Android: Settings › Display › Screen timeout › longest, and battery saver off.
  3. Volume all the way up and the phone plugged in.
  4. Yellow or orange tape over the flashlight.

Tap the screen any time to mute.
